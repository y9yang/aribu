# Aribu

Aribu maps floodwater from Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 optical imagery with U-Nets (neural networks that label every pixel of an image), and estimates how many people live in the flooded areas.

Source code: https://github.com/y9yang/aribu

Data: Sen1Floods11 · Copernicus Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 data · Copernicus Emergency Management Service © European Union · WorldPop · GHS-POP R2023A · geoBoundaries · JRC Global Surface Water.
